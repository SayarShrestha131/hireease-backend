# Profile Picture Upload Guide

## Requirements

- Format: JPEG or PNG
- Max size: 5MB
- Must contain a clearly visible face
- Minimum resolution: 200x200px

## Good Photo Tips

✅ **Good:**
- Face clearly visible and centered
- Good lighting (no harsh shadows)
- Looking directly at camera
- Plain or simple background

❌ **Avoid:**
- Sunglasses or hats covering face
- Blurry or low-resolution images
- Group photos
- Heavy filters or edits

## Face Validation

Your photo is automatically checked for a detectable face. If rejected:
1. Ensure your face is fully visible
2. Improve lighting
3. Try a different photo

## Privacy

Your profile picture is used solely for identity verification during KYC. It is stored securely and only accessible to authorized admins during the review process.

## Troubleshooting

| Error | Solution |
|-------|----------|
| "No face detected" | Ensure face is clearly visible and well-lit |
| "File too large" | Compress image below 5MB |
| "Invalid format" | Use JPEG or PNG only |
| "Too many uploads" | Wait 1 hour before retrying (5 uploads/hour limit) |
